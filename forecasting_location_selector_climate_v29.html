FORECASTING LOCATION SELECTOR

U.S. ZIP and Canadian FSA centroid placement, directional coverage and redundancy screening

Lean keeps only the minimum locations needed to meet the distance rule. Balanced also preserves useful directional coverage. Conservative tightens the working limits to about 4 miles normally and 2 miles in sensitive areas. Directional weighting controls how strongly unique north, south, east, west and diagonal positions are favored.
15-YEAR CLIMATE DIFFERENCE SCREEN
Nearby locations can be kept separate when their long-term snow or ice climatology differs enough to suggest distinct forecasting behavior.
A metric must exceed both the percentage and absolute difference to be considered significant. Climate screening is applied only when both locations have data.
